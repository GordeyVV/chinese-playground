  • The smaller 10-page characters PDF is a lesson 1 excerpt, not a second workbook. It matches full-workbook PDF 13–22.
  • The 27-page grammar handout expands lessons 1–12. It contains overgeneralizations and some numerical/pinyin errors, corrected in the companion explanations. Lessons 13–14 are supported by the book/decks.

Homework and answers / Задания и ответы

The digital workbook includes source-linked prompts, all original pinyin-sentence models, target-character readings, character meanings, phrase/sentence/dialogue completion, reading answers, sentence-making models and Reviews 1–2. Images and long passages remain in the learner’s locally opened original PDF. Copying tasks have a writing workflow and answer guide instead of a fabricated score.

No publisher answer key was found. Solutions are authored models checked against source questions. Open answers and full pinyin sentences use self-check; exact-answer tasks use normalized comparison. Known ambiguous reading options are accepted and explained. Revealing an answer prevents it counting later as an unaided first success.

Это не официальный ключ издателя. Ответы составлены и сверены с заданиями. Там, где допустимы синонимы, разные порядки слов или варианты записи тонов, используется сверка с образцом. Полные рисунки и тексты открываются из локального PDF. Копирование и каллиграфия не получают выдуманной оценки.

Learning and adaptation / Обучение и адаптация

  • Recall before reveal, correction after commitment, and review across days are the central loop. Spacing intervals are an explicit heuristic, not a validated learner model.
  • Grammar is explained through sentence structure and meaning contrasts, including likely Russian/English transfer mistakes. Component mnemonics are learning cues, not invented etymology.
  • Reading, listening comprehension, chunk ordering, written retrieval, handwriting and spoken production have different tasks. Practising one does not imply mastery of another.
  • Writing uses bundled Hanzi Writer animations and guided strokes, followed by a freehand/paper recall step. Speaking uses recording, a model, and self/partner comparison. Automatic transcripts are never pronunciation scores.
  • Adaptive priority: overdue cards, repeated checked errors, new words from started classes, then the next class. Every lesson remains accessible; the learner controls the class checklist.
  • Analytics report actual events, checked accuracy and sample size with descriptive Wilson intervals. Repeated observations are dependent; intervals are not proficiency or retention forecasts.

Главный цикл: вспомнить → проверить → исправить → повторить позже. Статистика разделяет проверенные ответы, самооценки и письмо по подсказке. Рекомендация объясняет, почему нужно повторение; приложение не присваивает уровень HSK по небольшой выборке.

Practical limits / Практические ограничения

A static GitHub Pages app has no cross-device account sync: export/import JSON moves progress. PDF files are private browser-local attachments and are not bundled for public redistribution. Device TTS depends on Mandarin voice availability. Microphone recording requires permission and a secure context. Optional speech recognition and external courses require network access; some course content may require enrollment/payment. The presentation media audit found 194 legacy Flash character animations and one photo document; no class-dialogue recording was recovered. Short character-animation sounds remain unverified and are not used as model audio. Offline support covers the bundled app after its first complete load, not external materials.

Прогресс хранится на устройстве; перенос — через JSON. PDF подключаются локально. Голос зависит от системы, микрофон требует разрешения и HTTPS. Внешние материалы могут требовать регистрации или оплаты. Офлайн работает само приложение после первой загрузки, но не внешние курсы.
